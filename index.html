About

A Florida LLC building a Cybercab-ready fleet hub

Cyber Fleet Miami LLC is a Florida limited liability company established to develop and operate an autonomous taxi fleet oriented around purpose-built Cybercab-class vehicles for the Miami metro and broader South Florida market.

Our focus is the operational layer: yard staging, charging readiness, vehicle readiness workflows, and a lean office footprint that can scale with fleet size — not consumer ride-hailing apps or speculative tech demos.

Fleet vision

Purpose-built autonomous cabs, staged overnight

Planned fleet framing — targets for a first operational wave, not vehicles currently in public service under this LLC.

01

Initial wave

A planned first tranche of 10–20 purpose-built autonomous cabs sized for Miami density, airport corridors, and nightlife demand patterns.

02

Overnight staging

Secure yard staging for end-of-shift parking, inspection, and dispatch prep — designed so vehicles return ready for the next operating window.

03

L2 charging

Level 2 charging capacity planned on-site so overnight dwell time doubles as energy recovery without relying on public fast-charge queues.

Operations

Yard + small office on the Allapattah / Medley corridor

Operational planning centers on a compact yard and office footprint along the Allapattah / Medley corridor — close enough to Miami’s core demand zones for efficient deadhead, with industrial land suited to overnight staging and charging.

  • Secure overnight vehicle staging
  • Light maintenance and readiness checks
  • Small ops office for dispatch coordination
  • Room to grow charging bays with fleet size
